What a pilot looks like

Illustrative examples of the kind of gap we find and the kind of result a 90-day pilot can show.

HVACIllustrative example
A 4-truck HVAC company with three senior techs eligible to retire in the next two years
The gap

Capturing what senior techs know before it walks out the door

Decades of know-how on quirky legacy installs lived only in a few technicians' heads, with no system of record — every upcoming retirement was a slow-motion callback problem.

What changed

Piloted an internal, company-only knowledge base that captures senior techs' site notes and troubleshooting shortcuts in plain language, searchable by address and equipment — nothing shared outside the company's own systems.

$38,000est. avoided callback cost / yr
22 hrssenior-tech time saved / mo
Customer impact: junior techs resolve legacy-install issues on the first visit instead of phoning a retired tech at home.
HVACIllustrative example
A 6-truck HVAC company doing about 50 installs a month, increasingly inverter/variable-speed systems
The gap

Inverter callbacks were quietly eating the install margin

Techs undertrained on inverter and variable-speed systems generated a disproportionate share of callbacks — comfort complaints and misread error codes a standard split system rarely produces.

What changed

Piloted an AI diagnostic-assist tool that walks a tech through inverter-specific fault trees and manufacturer bulletins from the truck, in real time.

$24,500est. reduced truck-roll cost / yr
31 hrscallback time saved / mo
Customer impact: comfort complaints drop because the true root cause gets fixed on the first visit.
PlumbingIllustrative example
A 5-truck plumbing company handling roughly 30 emergency water-damage calls a month
The gap

A slow dispatch call turns a repair into a claim

The gap between a same-day fix and a two-day delay was the single biggest driver of claim severity, but dispatch had no way to flag a high-risk job before it escalated.

What changed

Piloted an AI dispatch-prioritization step that flags active-water-loss calls for same-day routing based on the job description and photos submitted at intake.

$52,000est. avoided severity escalation / yr
14 hrsdispatcher triage time saved / mo
Customer impact: fewer emergency calls turn into full remediation or insurance claims.
PlumbingIllustrative example
A 4-truck plumbing company quoting 15–20 repipe and renovation jobs a month
The gap

Copper prices moved faster than the quote did

Copper and fixture prices shift week to week, so a quote written a few days before a job started often undershot actual material cost — eating straight into the margin.

What changed

Piloted an AI quoting-assist tool tied to a live materials price feed that flags any outstanding quote at risk of drift before it's sent.

$19,000est. recovered margin / yr
9 hrsre-quoting time saved / mo
Customer impact: fewer "the price went up" conversations once the crew is already on site.
ElectricalIllustrative example
A 5-crew electrical contractor doing 20–25 panel-upgrade estimates a month
The gap

Hand-run load calcs missed circuits added over the years

Load calculations done by hand at the estimate stage routinely missed circuits added to a property over time, turning a chunk of "routine" panel upgrades into a bigger, unbudgeted job once work started.

What changed

Piloted an AI-assisted load-calc check that cross-references the panel photo and the customer's described circuits against code before the estimate goes out.

$27,000est. avoided change-order disputes / yr
18 hrsrework time saved / mo
Customer impact: fewer surprise costs sprung on customers mid-job, fewer disputed invoices.
ElectricalIllustrative example
A 6-crew electrical contractor working across 8 neighboring towns
The gap

One shared playbook instead of tribal memory of what each town requires

Each town's inspector enforced slightly different, locally-amended code requirements — knowledge that lived only in whichever crew lead had worked that town before. New crews learned the hard way, with rejected inspections.

What changed

Piloted a shared, searchable playbook of jurisdiction-specific requirements built from past inspection notes — kept entirely in the company's own systems.

$15,500est. avoided re-inspection cost / yr
12 hrsinspection-prep time saved / mo
Customer impact: more jobs pass inspection on the first try, and close faster.
Dental & MedicalIllustrative example
A 3-location dental practice submitting roughly 400 insurance claims a month
The gap

The same denial reasons kept coming back

A recurring, predictable set of denial reasons — coding mismatches, missing attachments — ate front-desk hours in appeals and resubmissions every single week.

What changed

Piloted an AI claim-scrubbing check run before submission that flags the same denial patterns the practice has hit before, with a person reviewing every flag.

$41,000est. claims recovered / written off less / yr
26 hrsfront-desk rework saved / mo
Customer impact: patients see fewer surprise balance-due letters months after their visit.
Dental & MedicalIllustrative example
A 2-location medical practice processing prior authorizations for imaging and specialist referrals
The gap

Physicians were spending clinical hours on paperwork

Physicians and staff spent real clinical hours each week compiling records for prior-authorization requests — time that came directly out of patient-facing capacity.

What changed

Piloted an AI-assisted intake that pre-fills the prior-authorization packet from the chart, leaving just a physician review and sign-off.

$33,000est. value of physician time recovered / yr
11 hrsstaff time saved / wk
Customer impact: patients get an answer on imaging or specialist approval in days, not a week of back-and-forth.
Law FirmsIllustrative example
A 6-attorney general-practice firm running lean on paralegal support
The gap

Attorneys were absorbing unbillable admin work

With paralegal hiring tight, attorneys had started absorbing unbillable administrative work — formatting, filing prep, routine correspondence — that used to go to support staff.

What changed

Piloted an AI drafting-assist tool for routine correspondence and filing prep, with every draft reviewed and signed off by the attorney before it went out.

$58,000est. billable hours recovered / yr
9 hrsattorney time saved / wk
Customer impact: attorneys spend more of the week on billable client work instead of catching up on admin.
Law FirmsIllustrative example
A firm handling 2–3 active litigation matters with document sets in the tens of thousands of pages
The gap

First-pass document review was slow, expensive, and behind schedule

Document review at that volume, billed at associate rates, was slow and costly — and delays kept pushing discovery deadlines closer to the wire.

What changed

Piloted an AI-assisted first-pass review that flags likely-relevant documents for attorney review, with every flag checked by a human before it counts.

$71,000est. saved vs. associate-hour review cost
140 hrsfirst-pass review time saved
Customer impact: discovery deadlines are easier to hit without expanding the review team.
